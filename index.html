Research Interest

My earlier research focused on developing lightweight and deployable machine learning systems, particularly computer vision under resource constraints for medical imaging and environmental sensing. My current interests are efficient language model systems, reliable AI, reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making, and graph representation learning. Across these areas, I ask how learning systems can use limited data and computation well, and what evidence supports their decisions. I also continue my work in medical imaging.
