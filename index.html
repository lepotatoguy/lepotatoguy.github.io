Joyanta J. Mondal

he / himM.S. in Computer ScienceDallas, TX, USA

I am a researcher from Dhaka, Bangladesh, currently based in Dallas, Texas, with an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Delaware.

My earlier research focused on developing lightweight and deployable machine learning systems for real-world problems. Much of it was in computer vision under resource constraints, with applications in medical imaging and environmental sensing. I'm now extending this focus toward trustworthy and reliable AI, exploring how foundation models and learning systems can be adapted and evaluated to improve their reliability, with particular interests in parameter-efficient fine-tuning, evidence-grounded reasoning and memory in large language models, and reliable model evaluation. I'm also interested in reinforcement learning for structured learning and decision-making.

My Erdős number is 4.

Outside research: avid gamer (known by the in-game name LePotatoGuy), anime and comic enthusiast, cyclist, and a chess player rated ~400 (which means I lose often and confidently). Feel free to test me anyway.
