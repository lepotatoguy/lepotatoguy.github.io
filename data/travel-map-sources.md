Travel map boundaries

US: US Atlas 3.0.1, states-albers-10m.json. Derived from US Census Bureau cartographic state boundaries, 2017. Albers USA projection with Alaska and Hawaii insets. District of Columbia and territories excluded.
Source: https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas

Bangladesh: geoBoundaries gbOpen BGD ADM2, boundary ID BGD-ADM2-16705992, commit 9469f09. Authority: Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), OCHA ROAP. License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO.
Source: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/BGD/ADM2/
Geometry: https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/BGD/ADM2/geoBoundaries-BGD-ADM2_simplified.geojson
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/

Adaptations: simplified source geometry converted to SVG path coordinates using d3-geo; Bangladesh rendered with Mercator projection. Screen coordinates rounded to one decimal. No boundary changes intended. Rendering data stored in travel-regions.json.

Visits: transcribed from the two user-supplied travel map images on 2026-10-14: 22 US states and 22 Bangladesh districts. District names match the geometry dataset spellings (Chittagong, Comilla, Jessore). Oregon and Washington added on 2026-10-26 from the user's confirmation. Current totals: 24 US states and 22 Bangladesh districts. Update travel-visits.json and the HTML text fallback together when more places are added.
